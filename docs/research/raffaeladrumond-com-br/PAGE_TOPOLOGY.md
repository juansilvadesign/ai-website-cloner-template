## Responsive rules

- **< 720px:** 20px outer gutters, one-column editorial grids, menu contact details simplify, headings approach 42px/28px tiers, and images maintain rounded 20px framing.
- **720–1023px:** two-column opportunities return where image density allows; header retains compact controls.
- **>= 1024px:** 1200px container, 90px section rhythm, 64px editorial headings, alternating treatment groups, and desktop header contact actions.

## Layering and containment

- Hero image is a full-bleed background with its foreground copy constrained to the shared content width.
- The menu is fixed across the viewport and receives a dark neutral scrim.
- Decorative botanical marks are absolutely layered behind the criterion content.
- The WhatsApp shortcut is fixed at the lower edge, above document content.
