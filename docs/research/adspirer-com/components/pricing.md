# Pricing

- **Default state:** annual billing. Plans are Free ($0), Plus ($40 annual / $49 monthly), Pro ($83 / $99), and Max ($167 / $199).
- **Control:** 237×50px rounded gray segmented picker, annual option conveys “−17%”; active pill is white with a soft 1px shadow.
- **Desktop:** four neighboring plan cards plus a wide Enterprise callout; card corners 16px, borders subtle, selected growth plan may be visually emphasized.
- **Phone:** cards stack without hiding comparison information. Prices, save labels, CTA destinations, and features update together on billing switch.
