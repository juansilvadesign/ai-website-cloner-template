# Featured product cards

- **Role:** demonstrate concrete account work before explaining configuration.
- **Desktop composition:** lead-in headline occupies the first row; a large campaign-build card sits left, a smaller “Works where you work” card right, followed by two compact cards.
- **Phone:** cards become a single stacked narrative and retain their 16–20px rounded corners.
- **Surface rules:** white panels, subtle `rgba(16,24,40,.10)` outline, 16px radius, feathered blue/lilac/orange internal gradients. Screens and phone mockups should be clipped inside panels.
